## Who we are
- Driven runs basketball training, teams and summer camps for kids and teens.

## Philosophy
- Every player gets lots of touches: small groups, short lines, no one standing for long.
- Compete every day: keep score in drills when it helps effort.
- Teach the why: one or two coaching points per drill, not ten.

## Camp rules
- Water break at least every 15–20 minutes, more in heat.
- Group campers by age and size for any contact or competitive game.
- Every game needs a version that works for a group of 8 and a group of 30.
